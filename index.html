About TALENTMATCH

Hackathon Prototype

TALENTMATCH is an intelligent resume and job description matching system designed to help recruiters process large volumes of candidate applications quickly, objectively, and transparently.

Core Problem & Solution

Recruiters often spend hours skimming resumes, leading to keyword-matching bias and overlooked talent. TALENTMATCH replaces static keyword matching with a 4-factor NLP scoring engine and evidence consistency auditing.

Key Features

  • Transparent Match Score (0–100%): Calculated as Skill Match (40%), Experience (25%), Education (10%), and Semantic NLP relevance (25%).
  • Truth / Confidence Score (0–100%): Audits internal document evidence consistency without labeling candidates negatively.
  • Explainable Recommendations: Automatically classifies candidates into Strongly Recommended, Recommended, Consider, or Not Recommended with clear rationales.
  • Skill Gap Identification: Visually highlights matched required skills, missing required skills, and additional skills.
  • Zero-Dependency Local Engine: Parses PDF, DOCX, and TXT files directly in-browser with zero paid API dependencies.

Privacy & Demonstration Note

Uploaded resume data is processed for candidate analysis in this hackathon prototype. Avoid uploading sensitive personal information during public demonstrations.
